Tracking Local SEO success means measuring three things at once: rankings in searches by region, actions generated on Google Business Profile (views, calls, direction requests), and real conversions on the site or by phone. Rankings alone aren't enough: a good position that doesn't generate a call or a visit to the physical store doesn't justify the investment. Whether you're a restaurant in São Paulo, a dental clinic in Rio de Janeiro, or a building-materials store in Belo Horizonte, your local online presence can be the difference between attracting new customers or losing them to competitors.

## Local rankings: what actually matters

(Image: screenshot of a Google search for "restaurants in Curitiba" showing the local pack)

When we talk about Local SEO, Google rankings are often the first metric that comes to mind. And rightly so! Ranking well when someone searches for services in your region is essential.

That said, it's important to understand that local rankings can vary significantly depending on:

-   **Exact search location** – Results for "bakery" in Copacabana differ from results in Leblon, even though they're close neighborhoods in Rio de Janeiro.
-   **User's search history** – If a user has already visited your site or interacted with your business, they may see personalized results.
-   **Device used** – Mobile searches can show different results than desktop searches.

That's why, when [tracking local rankings](/en/blog/como-melhorar-o-ranking-do-seu-site-no-google-dicas-praticas/), it's essential to account for these variables and not fixate on the numerical position alone. Having the [right local keywords](/en/blog/como-escolher-as-melhores-palavras-chave-para-seu-negocio-local/) mapped out from the start makes this tracking much easier.

## What tools monitor Local SEO in Brazil?

Several tools can help you monitor your Local SEO performance:

-   **[RD Station](https://www.rdstation.com/)** – Popular among Brazilian companies, it offers features for tracking rankings and organic traffic.
-   **Semrush** – Lets you monitor rankings across different locations and devices.
-   **[Moz Local](https://moz.com/products/local)** – Useful for managing local citations and monitoring information consistency.
-   **[BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/)** – Specialized in Local SEO, with features built for businesses with a physical presence.

Also see our guide with [more free SEO monitoring tools](/en/blog/como-monitorar-desempenho-seo-seu-site-ferramentas-gratuitas/).

## Which metrics actually matter beyond rankings?

Conversion metrics (calls, bookings, visits to the physical store) matter more than an isolated ranking position, because they measure whether the search turned into business. Rankings remain relevant as a visibility indicator, but they're only a means to Local SEO's real end: generating revenue.

That's why it's essential to track metrics closer to conversion:

### 1. Google Business Profile traffic

[Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811?hl=en) offers valuable insight into how customers find your business:

-   **Profile views** – How many people saw your listing on Google?
-   **Actions** – How many people clicked to call, get directions, or visit your site?
-   **Directions** – Which neighborhoods or cities are people requesting directions from?

A furniture store in Porto Alegre, for example, might discover it's attracting customers from Canoas or Gravataí, which could shape future strategy.

### 2. Phone calls

For many Brazilian businesses, especially small ones like auto repair shops, restaurants, or hair salons, phone calls are a direct indicator of success.

You can track calls in a few different ways:

-   **Dedicated numbers per channel** – Use one number for your site, another for your Google Business Profile, and so on.
-   **Call tracking services** – Companies like Calltrack Brasil, or resources like Magazine Luiza Connect for retailers.
-   **Asking on the call** – Simply asking how the customer found your business can provide valuable insight.

### 3. On-site conversions

If your local site allows bookings, quote requests, or other actions, it's important to track these conversions:

-   **Forms submitted** – How many people request quotes or information?
-   **Online bookings** – If you're a medical practice or a hair salon, for example, how many bookings come through the site?
-   **Online chat** – Tools like [JivoChat](https://www.jivochat.com.br/) or Zendesk, popular in Brazil, let you track conversations started through the site.

## How do you tie Local SEO to real sales?

The final challenge is connecting your Local SEO efforts to real sales. Some strategies for this include:

-   **Asking customers** – Train your team at Magazine Luiza, Casas Bahia, or your small local store to ask how customers found the business.
-   **Channel-specific promo codes** – Offer an exclusive coupon on your Google Business Profile, different from what you offer on other channels.
-   **CRM integration** – Systems like Pipedrive or RD Station CRM, popular among Brazilian companies, can help trace lead origin all the way to final conversion.

## Conclusion: an ongoing process

Local SEO isn't a "set it and forget it" strategy. Three tracking fronts sustain the result: rankings by region and device, actions on Google Business Profile (views, calls, direction requests), and attributed conversions (forms, bookings, tracked calls). It's an ongoing process that requires [constant monitoring and data-driven adjustments](/en/blog/como-melhorar-o-ranking-do-seu-site-no-google-dicas-praticas/), as shown by the cases of the inn in Florianópolis and the pharmacy chain in Salvador.

In Brazil's competitive market, ranking well in local search and being able to prove that position through calls and real sales is what separates businesses that grow from businesses that merely show up on the map.
