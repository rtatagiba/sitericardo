**[Local SEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_engine_optimization) is the set of optimization practices that helps businesses with a physical address show up in searches from people nearby, and it's essential for small businesses that serve a specific region.** Nearly 46% of all Google searches already carry local intent, and 72% of consumers who run a local search visit a nearby physical store shortly after, which makes this optimization one of the investments with the most direct return for anyone who depends on customers from their area.

Below are seven work streams for physical businesses — stores, restaurants, clinics, service providers, and so on — covering keyword research, on-page optimization, local content, Google Business Profile, backlinks, competitor analysis, and "near me" FAQs. Each section explains the logic behind the tactic and includes a ready-to-use AI prompt.

### 1. How do you research local keywords?

Researching local keywords starts with identifying [geographically focused keywords](/en/blog/como-escolher-as-melhores-palavras-chave-para-seu-negocio-local/) before optimizing any page.

For example, instead of optimizing just for "pizzeria," using "pizzeria in Campinas" makes it clear to Google and to users where your business is, which increases your relevance. Geo-targeted keywords have a significant impact — they can increase revenue by up to 333%, thanks to the higher purchase intent of people searching locally.

Use ChatGPT to generate local keyword ideas, including long-tail variations with neighborhoods, landmarks, and regional terms used by customers in your area.

**Prompts for this task:**

-   "I'm a business owner in [City]. Generate a list of local keywords I can target to improve my online visibility, sorted from most important to least important."
-   "What search terms do customers in [City] use to find [product/service]? List 10 keyword suggestions, including neighborhood names or regional expressions specific to [City]."

### 2. How do you optimize on-page elements for local SEO?

On-page optimization with a local focus makes sure every page on your site makes clear which locality it serves. Include the city or region name in key page elements, like the title tag, meta description, headings (H1), and body copy.

For example, instead of a generic title like "Artisan Burger," use something like "Artisan Burger in São Paulo – Fast Delivery" in the page title. Likewise, work the city into descriptions and into at least one subheading or paragraph of the content.

Studies recommend adding the city/neighborhood to every core content element — title, H1, URL, body text, and even image alt tags — at least once each. This signals to search engines and users where the service is offered, boosting relevance in local search.

Also check that your name, address, and phone number (NAP) appear on the site (for example, in the footer or contact page), since NAP consistency contributes to [Local SEO](/en/blog/sucesso-em-seo-local-como-acompanhar-rankings-conversoes-e-chamadas/). Ideally, reinforce this information with [LocalBusiness structured data](/en/blog/o-poder-do-schema-org-para-empresas-de-servicos-um-guia-completo-para-o-seo-local/).

**Prompts:**

-   "Analyze the page [page URL or 'my service page'] on my site and point out on-page SEO improvements with a local focus. Consider optimizations to the title, meta description, headings, and use of [City] in the content."
-   "Write an attractive SEO title and an optimized meta description for the [service] page in [City], including the local keyword and a call-to-action for users in the area."

### 3. How do you create locally focused blog content?

Publishing content relevant to the local community is an excellent [SEO strategy](/en/blog/como-garantir-orcamento-para-seo-estrategias-para-justificar-investimentos-em-marketing-organico/). Besides demonstrating authority, it attracts visitors from the region and puts your site in front of more specific searches.

Focus on local themes: you can create city guides, neighborhood news, event roundups, or "best of [city]" lists. For example, a restaurant could publish an article like "The 5 Most Ordered Dishes in Curitiba," or a candy shop could write "Best Desserts for Parties in Rio de Janeiro."

This kind of content highlights local context and helps Google notice you're active in the community, while also engaging residents and potential customers. ChatGPT can help by suggesting blog topics, building local editorial calendars, or even drafting posts optimized with keywords from the region.

Remember to promote these posts locally too (neighborhood social media, city groups) to expand their reach.

**Prompt examples:**

-   "Suggest 5 blog post ideas focused on [industry/topic] that could attract customers from [City]."
-   "Create a detailed outline for a blog article about [topic/service], aimed at readers in [City]. Include sections in the outline that highlight specific points about the city (e.g., neighborhoods, local events) and incorporate relevant regional keywords."

### 4. How do you optimize your Google Business Profile?

Having a well-optimized [Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/) is essential for local SEO. Make sure to fill in every field: full address, phone number, hours of operation, website, business category, description, and quality photos of the establishment.

Complete profiles have a much better chance of attracting visits — customers are 70% more likely to visit businesses with a complete, up-to-date Google Business Profile. Keep everything current: if your hours or address change, update it immediately, and take the opportunity to add updates (recent photos, new services, event/promo posts) regularly.

Encourage customers to leave reviews and respond to all of them, positive or negative, professionally — this improves your reputation and shows engagement. Also use the Q&A section on the profile to clear up common questions (you can add frequently asked questions yourself and answer them).

**Prompt examples:**

-   "Analyze my business's Google Business Profile. Business: [Name], Category: [category], Location: [City]. What improvements can I make to optimize this profile for local SEO?"
-   "List practical suggestions to improve my Google Business Profile: from information I should add or fix, to recommended photo types, use of posts, and strategies for getting more customer reviews."

### 5. How do you earn local backlinks?

Backlinks (links from other sites pointing to yours) remain an important authority factor in SEO, and in a local context, the local quality of those links matters a lot.

Focus on local partnerships: look for link opportunities on sites belonging to organizations in your city (chambers of commerce, neighborhood associations, local news portals, regional blogs, event sponsors). List your business in trusted local directories (city guides, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Google Maps, etc.) and keep the data consistent across all of them — this reinforces the credibility of your business information.

Another tactic is the local guest post: offer to write an article for local partner blogs or sites in exchange for a link. Local networking is essential: attend industry events in your region, engage in community groups online and offline — these contacts often generate natural mentions and links to your site.

Use ChatGPT to brainstorm local link-building sites and strategies, and even to draft initial outreach emails.

**Prompt examples:**

-   "List ideas for how to earn local backlinks for a [type of business] in [City]."
-   "What local sites or organizations could I reach out to for [backlinks to my business](/en/blog/como-melhorar-o-ranking-do-seu-site-no-google-dicas-praticas/) in [City]? Also suggest how to approach each one to request a backlink."

### 6. How do you analyze local competitors?

Understanding competitors' strategies in your city can reveal valuable opportunities, a process [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/library/seo) covers frequently in its SEO analyses. Check who ranks in the top positions for your industry's local keywords and analyze their strengths: site content, use of local keywords, backlink profile, Google Business Profile presence (number of reviews, average rating), and more.

Evaluating competitors' content and spotting gaps or areas for improvement is an essential practice. Use SEO tools (like Semrush, Ahrefs, Moz) to find out which local keywords your rivals rank for and which sites link to them.

ChatGPT can help by summarizing this data and even generating comparative reports if you feed it the information. Learn from the competition: see what's working for them in your region and think about how to do it better or differently.

**Prompt examples:**

-   "Identify my business's main competitors (industry: [your industry], location: [City]) in local search results. Compare each competitor's SEO strengths and point out where my site can improve to outrank them."
-   "Analyze the local SEO strategies of my 3 biggest competitors in [City]. List insights on keywords, local content, backlinks, and Google Business Profile reputation. Suggest actions to help me stand out."

### 7. How do you answer "near me" searches with FAQs?

"Near me" searches have exploded in popularity in recent years. That signals users want immediate solutions close to their current location.

One strategy is to create an FAQ section aimed at your local audience. Think about the common questions your regional customers have and answer them, including geographic references.

For example: "Do you have a location near me in [City]?" — and then explain that you serve the whole city, mentioning key neighborhoods. Also use the Q&A section on your Google Business Profile.

Avoid forcing the phrase "near me" artificially. Instead, use natural variations like "in the [neighborhood] area," "close to downtown [City]," and so on.

**Prompt examples:**

-   "Create an FAQ section for the website of a [type of business] in [City]. Include ~5 clear questions and answers that address local customers' concerns."
-   "Generate questions and answers including the phrase 'near me' for a [type of business] in [City], explaining how my business meets that need."

### Summary: the 7 local SEO fronts to tackle first

A small business serving a specific region gains visibility by combining these fronts:

-   **Local keywords**: using "pizzeria in Campinas" instead of "pizzeria" can boost revenue by up to 333%, thanks to the higher purchase intent of local searchers.
-   **Local on-page**: city/neighborhood in the title, H1, URL, and content, with consistent NAP (name, address, phone) reinforced by [LocalBusiness structured data](/en/blog/o-poder-do-schema-org-para-empresas-de-servicos-um-guia-completo-para-o-seo-local/).
-   **Local blog content**: neighborhood guides, regional news, and "best of [city]" lists build authority and bring in qualified traffic.
-   **Google Business Profile**: complete, up-to-date profiles increase the likelihood of a visit by 70%.
-   **Local backlinks**: partnerships, directories, and regional guest posts are worth more than generic, low-relevance links.
-   **Competitor analysis**: tools like Semrush, Ahrefs, and Moz reveal which local terms rivals already rank for.
-   **"Near me" FAQs**: direct answers with natural variations ("in the [neighborhood] area") capture these searches without sounding forced.
