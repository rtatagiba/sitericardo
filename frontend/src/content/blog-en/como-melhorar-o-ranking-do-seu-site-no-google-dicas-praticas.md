Google handles more than 90% of the world's internet searches, making it the primary organic traffic channel for most sites. Appearing in the top search results is essential to gaining visibility, driving qualified traffic, and ultimately, conversions for your online business. This article covers effective strategies and techniques for improving your site's position in Google's search results.

### What Is Ranking on Google

Search engine ranking, especially on Google, refers to the position your site occupies in search results for a given query. Google uses a complex algorithm made up of more than 200 factors to determine which pages are most relevant and useful for each specific query.

These results are shown in descending order of relevance, from the most relevant page (position #1) to the least. Studies show that more than 75% of users never go past the first page of results, and the top three results capture more than 60% of all clicks.

### Factors That Influence Ranking

### On-Page Factors

On-page factors are elements within your site that can be optimized to improve ranking:

-   **Quality content**: Useful, original, well-structured information
    
-   **Keyword optimization**: Strategic use of relevant terms
    
-   **Meta tags**: Title, description, and heading tags (H1, H2, etc.)
    
-   **URL structure**: Friendly, descriptive URLs
    
-   **Optimized images**: Image compression and alt attribute usage
    
-   **Time on page**: How long users stay on your page
    

### Off-Page Factors

Off-page factors are elements external to your site:

-   **Backlinks**: Links from other sites pointing to yours
    
-   **Domain authority**: Your site's overall reputation
    
-   **Social signals**: Shares and mentions on social media
    
-   **Local citations**: Mentions of your business in local directories (for local SEO)
    
-   **Link profile**: The diversity and quality of the links you receive
    

### Technical Factors

Technical aspects that affect site performance:

-   **Loading speed**: How long your page takes to load
    
-   **Mobile-friendliness**: Adaptation for mobile devices
    
-   **Indexability**: How easily Google finds and indexes your pages
    
-   **Security**: HTTPS implementation
    
-   **Site structure**: Information architecture and internal navigation
    
-   **Structured data**: Schema markup implementation

### Google Algorithm Updates

Google rolls out hundreds of algorithm updates a year, some more significant than others. The main recent updates include:

1.  **Core Updates**: Regular updates that adjust various aspects of the algorithm.
    
2.  **Page Experience Update**: Focused on user experience metrics, including Core Web Vitals.
    
3.  **BERT and MUM**: Updates to natural language and context understanding.
    
4.  **Helpful Content Update**: Prioritizes content created primarily for people, not for search engines.
    
5.  **Link Spam Update**: Identifies and devalues manipulative links.
    

Stay up to date on algorithm changes so you can adapt your strategy when needed.

### SEO Best Practices for 2025

Current SEO trends point to the importance of:

1.  **Search-intent-driven content**: Understanding and precisely addressing what the user is looking for.
    
2.  **[Semantic SEO and entities](/en/blog/a-mudanca-para-o-seo-semantico-o-que-os-vetores-significam-para-sua-estrategia/)**: Focusing more on meaning and context than on isolated keywords.
    
3.  **Artificial intelligence and machine learning**: Using AI to build more efficient content strategies.
    
4.  **User experience as a top factor**: Sites that deliver a better experience tend to rank better.
    
5.  **Multimedia content**: Videos, podcasts, infographics, and other formats beyond text.
    
6.  **Voice search and zero-click searches**: Optimizing for direct answers and featured snippets.
    
7.  **E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)**: Demonstrating experience, knowledge, authority, and trustworthiness.
    
8.  **Search intent analysis**: Understanding the context and purpose behind queries.

### Final Thoughts

Improving your site's ranking on Google requires a holistic approach that combines technical optimization, quality content, authority building, and an excellent user experience. There are no shortcuts — sustainable SEO takes consistency, patience, and constant adaptation to algorithm and user behavior changes.

Keep in mind that SEO results are rarely immediate. It generally takes 3 to 6 months to see meaningful ranking changes, especially in competitive niches. Stay focused on best practices and on creating genuine value for users.

Google keeps evolving its algorithm to identify and reward sites that offer the best answer to users' queries. So rather than chasing specific tactics, focus on understanding and meeting your target audience's needs.
